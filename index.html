About Our College

M. C. Varghese College of Arts & Science (Estd. 2011) is a fit memorial for the great visionary who left an indelible imprint on the sensibilities of his contemporary Malayalees through an impressive array of publications.

Initially opened in the Mangalam Press building at Neelimangalam, Kottayam, the college was shifted to modern premises at Mangalam Campus, Ettumanoor in 2014. Since its inception, the college is affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam.

Our Programs

Explore diverse programs designed to shape your future

BA Animation & Graphic Design (Hons)

Creative program focusing on 2D/3D animation, motion graphics, visual effects, and graphic design principles for digital media and entertainment industry. Master industry-standard tools like Maya, Blender, After Effects, and Photoshop. Curriculum covers character design, storyboarding, rigging, texturing, and compositing. Students work on animated shorts, game assets, and commercial projects. Career paths include animator, VFX artist, game designer, and creative director.

Duration: 3 Years

BCA (Hons)

Bachelor of Computer Applications with focus on programming, software development, database management, and emerging technologies like AI and machine learning. Learn programming languages including Java, Python, C++, and web technologies. Curriculum covers data structures, algorithms, mobile app development, cloud computing, and cybersecurity. Includes industry internships and live project work. Graduates become software developers, system analysts, web developers, and IT consultants.

Duration: 3 Years

BBA (Hons)

Bachelor of Business Administration covering management principles, marketing, finance, human resources, and entrepreneurship with practical business case studies. Curriculum includes strategic management, digital marketing, international business, and business analytics. Students participate in corporate internships, business plan competitions, and industry mentorship programs. Prepares graduates for roles as business managers, marketing executives, HR specialists, and entrepreneurs.

Duration: 3 Years

B.Com Computer Application (Hons)

Commerce degree integrated with computer applications, covering accounting software, business analytics, e-commerce, and digital business solutions. Students master Tally, SAP, QuickBooks, and advanced Excel for financial analysis. Curriculum includes digital marketing, online business models, ERP systems, and financial technology. Combines traditional commerce knowledge with modern IT skills. Career opportunities include financial analyst, accounts manager, e-commerce specialist, and business consultant.

Duration: 3 Years

B.Com Finance and Taxation (Hons)

Specialized commerce program focusing on financial management, taxation laws, investment analysis, and corporate finance with practical training in tax software. Comprehensive coverage of GST, income tax, corporate taxation, and international finance. Students gain expertise in financial planning, portfolio management, and tax compliance. Includes preparation for CA, CS, and CMA foundation exams. Graduates work as tax consultants, financial advisors, investment analysts, and chartered accountants.

Duration: 3 Years

M.Com Finance and Taxation

Advanced postgraduate program in commerce with specialization in finance and taxation, research methodology, and preparation for professional certifications. In-depth study of advanced financial theories, international taxation, forensic accounting, and financial risk management. Students conduct independent research projects and case studies. Program includes preparation for UGC NET, professional certifications, and doctoral studies. Career advancement opportunities in academia, research, senior financial positions, and consulting.

Duration: 2 Years

Frequently Asked Questions

What programs does Mangalam College offer?

We offer undergraduate programs including BCA (Hons), BBA (Hons), BA Animation & Graphic Design (Hons), B.Com Computer Application (Hons), B.Com Finance and Taxation (Hons), and postgraduate M.Com Finance and Taxation. All programs are affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University.

What is the admission process?

Admissions are based on merit and entrance examinations as per MG University guidelines. Applications can be submitted online or at our campus. Required documents include academic certificates, ID proof, and passport-size photographs.

What facilities are available on campus?

Our modern campus features digital classrooms, Wi-Fi connectivity, well-equipped library, computer labs, canteen, sports facilities, medical center, and separate hostel accommodations for boys and girls.

Are there placement opportunities?

Yes, we have a dedicated placement cell with 100% placement rate. We conduct regular campus recruitment drives, industry visits, internship programs, and career guidance sessions to ensure excellent job opportunities for our graduates.

What is the fee structure?

Fee structure varies by program. We offer flexible payment options and scholarship opportunities for deserving students. For detailed fee information, please contact our admissions office or visit our campus.

Where is the college located?

Mangalam College is located at Mangalam Campus, Ettumanoor, Kottayam, Kerala. The campus is just 3 km from Ettumanoor town with excellent transport connectivity and scenic surroundings.
